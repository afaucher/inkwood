Engagement cones, one per weapon

Proposed weapons and values (placeholders until the unit sheets), built on Alex's rules: a cone per weapon, the same cone in height, random-ish hits, and better odds near the centre for machine guns. Darker shading = better odds. Fixed guns are drawn peaked; flexible guns and turrets are proposed flatter, since a gunner aims within the arc.

Every gun sits on a hardpoint (Alex): a mount point on the airframe, in metres forward, right and up from the plane's centre, and its cone starts there. The close-ups mark them. Proposed option to weigh: a pair of wing guns angled in to meet at a set distance (real fighters did, at about 250 m), which would make the odds best at that range.

Roster

Each unit is fitted to its cell; the inked bar under it is 10 m. Aircraft fly at the altitude knob's height, so their shadows sit apart. Tap a unit for its next variant.

True scale

Every unit at one pixels-per-metre, beside the prototype's tree (19 px canopy) and house (28 px), which keep their own pixel size. The pixels-per-metre knob changes only this strip. It scrolls sideways when it is wider than the screen.

Unit definitions in metres (the chosen variant of each unit)

Fractions (nose, wing_at, canopy_at and the like) are fractions of the unit's length; angles are degrees; everything else is metres or a count. These are the numbers each silhouette is built from.

Sides proposed

One accent per side, on one zone only: wing roundels, a recognition panel on the tank's engine deck, the battery's ground panel, the cruiser's quarterdeck and the tower hut's roof. Proposed: brick red #A45A4E oklch(0.551 0.100 30) and slate blue #4E72AC oklch(0.551 0.100 260), equal lightness and chroma. The last roster cell shows both.

Ink

Pen line

How an ink line's width varies along it. Even: one width, as today. Tapered: hand pressure swells and thins the line and it tapers where the pen lands and lifts. Shadow side: outlines heavier on the edges facing away from the sun. Broad nib: width follows the stroke's direction.

Light

Scale and altitude

A true 600 m altitude would throw a plane's shadow 580 m away. Proposed: draw the shadow as if the plane flew at a fixed fraction of its altitude, so the gap still grows with height and shrinks as it descends.
